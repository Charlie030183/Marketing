Works with GitHub

The ScreenAntics project exists to keep Johnny Castaway alive in the days of Windows 11 and Linux, preserving the spirit, scenes, sounds and personality that made the screensaver memorable.

It is a tribute of gratitude to the creative developers of the original work. The goal is not to replace or reinvent Johnny, but to conserve the experience and adapt only what is necessary for it to keep working on modern computers.

The story of Johnny Castaway

Released in 1992 for Microsoft Windows 3.1, Screen Antics followed Johnny, a castaway living alone on a small island. Instead of repeating a simple animation, the screensaver presented small scenes from the character's daily life.

Those situations gradually formed a story. Visual jokes, routines, sounds and unexpected events made Johnny feel as if he kept living his life even when nobody was watching the screen.

What we are preserving

  • The original scenes and narrative.
  • The character's sounds, timing and behavior.
  • The randomness that makes each run different.
  • An Original mode as the fidelity reference.
  • Modern presentation options without replacing preserved content.
  • Compatibility with Windows 11 and, later, Linux.

Non-profit preservation

This is an independent, non-profit fan preservation project. Rights to the original work remain with their respective holders. The preservation effort aims to keep a memorable piece of software history accessible on new generations of computers.
