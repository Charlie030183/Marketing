SmailsMinimalist mobile email
Works with GitHub

Smails is a fast, minimalist email app for Android and iOS. It keeps the essentials in reach and hides the rest behind Advanced Options.

It is inspired by clean mail apps such as Spark, with a more three-dimensional paper-plane mark, light themes, readable type, and short animations.

  • Sign in first with Gmail; Outlook and other providers stay in Advanced Options.
  • No private mail server of its own — it talks to your existing accounts.
  • Messages arrive one by one so the inbox feels immediate.
  • Report spam, block a sender, and undo send for one minute.
  • Family sideload first; store listing only after Charlie approves.
