Works with GitHub

Smails improves everyday email use with a simple, fast and organized interface.

The app keeps the main functions on screen and moves less-used options into advanced areas, reducing visual clutter without removing important capabilities.

Its goal is to make email clearer and more pleasant for:

  • Signing in with Gmail easily and adding other providers through advanced options.
  • Reading, replying, searching and composing messages with fewer steps.
  • Synchronizing messages in the background and supporting offline use when possible.
  • Blocking senders, reporting spam and undoing sends.
  • Adapting the interface to phones, tablets and foldable devices.

These capabilities are developed with a focus on simplicity, speed, accessibility and low operating cost.

How Smails organizes email

Main functions remain accessible without excessive menus, while advanced settings stay available when needed.

  • Inbox — quick reading and navigation.
  • Compose — simple writing and sending flow.
  • Accounts — Gmail first, with expansion to other providers.
  • Adaptive interface — better use of different screen sizes and form factors.

Privacy and operation

Smails works as a client for the user's accounts and avoids proprietary infrastructure when it is not needed, reducing dependencies, costs and complexity.

Technologies used: Dart, Kotlin, YAML and JSON.
